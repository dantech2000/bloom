## Smoother playback

On 0.1.2, between 5 and 19% of the frames of a 24 fps file reached the screen one refresh late, which shows as small hitches in a pan. The frame was drawn too late in the refresh interval and left the GPU about 4 ms before the deadline of the display. Bloom now draws each frame earlier in the interval and adjusts that point from the times at which frames are in fact shown.

Measured on a 1080p movie, on a 60 Hz display: 384 of 7,196 frames late before, 7 of 7,180 after. Over a whole film: 183 of 184,850 (0.1%).

Other refresh rates (120 Hz and so on) are covered by simulated tests, not yet by a measurement on such a display.

## Playback fixes

- Closing the player while an item still loads no longer lets the sound start behind the closed player.
- The subtitle look, the audio and subtitle choice from the detail page, mute, volume and speed are kept on the first play after a start of the app.
- The trailer on the home page no longer stalls for up to 200 ms when it loads.
- After playback the app is quiet again: about 38 wakeups a second, down from 261.
- A change of quality while another one is under way no longer freezes the window.

## Your data

- A position of an offline play goes only to the profile and server it belongs to.
- "Remove" followed by "Download" no longer leaves the new download stuck.
- A download from one server never plays as the item of another server with the same id.
- A refused save of the server settings keeps your edits on the page, to correct and send again.
- A save of a plugin's settings can no longer land in the editor of another plugin.
- A bad answer of a proxy or a damaged picture is no longer kept in the image cache, and a bad file already in the cache is replaced.
- Settings saved in quick succession, or around a change of profile, reach the server in order.

## Pages

- Back opens a page that was left while it loaded, and loads it.
- Search no longer stays on "loading" after a quick correction of the text.
- A connection lost in the middle of an answer shows the server as offline.

## Chromecast, AirPlay, SyncPlay

- Chromecast: controls pressed during a short loss of the connection reach the device afterwards; a seek while paused stays paused; a device that does not answer fails with a message; a device that was unplugged leaves the list.
- AirPlay: a pause in the last half second no longer ends the session; a new session takes the mute and volume of the app.
- SyncPlay: the group clock is correct after the Mac slept.

## Smaller

The program inside the app is 27 MB, down from 42 MB. The download is 60 MB, as most of it is the bundled video libraries.
