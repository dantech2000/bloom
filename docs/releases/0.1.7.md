An installed 0.1.6 updates itself: it shows "Restart to update" when the download is ready.

Most of this release is work you do not see. Pages draw a little cheaper, many small faults are fixed, and two experimental features for automation are in Settings.

## Automation (experimental)

Settings > Automation is new. Both features are off until you turn them on.

- **Events for other programs.** Bloom tells other programs on this Mac what the player does: the start, pause, seek and end of a playback, the credits, full screen and picture in picture. An event never has your token, the server address, a file path or your user name. "Name the title in events" decides whether the title is in it.
- **Control lights.** A small helper dims the lights you choose while a film plays, gives a warm white at a pause, and puts back what each light had at the end. Or the lights follow the colours of the picture. It speaks to Govee lights on your network, with "LAN Control" on in the Govee Home app. It was tried on one model, the H6076.

## Faster pages

Pages with cards (the libraries, the page of a title, the search, the server dashboard) need 4 to 10% less time for a frame.

## Fixed

Playback and casting:

- A play that fails to load again leaves nothing playing in the background.
- A cast that gets no answer from the device gives up, and a new title is kept while the app on the device starts.
- An AirPlay route that fails after the start comes back to this Mac with a notice.
- A trailer plays web links only, and the end of an older trailer does not mark a newer one as failed.

Downloads:

- A file that was replaced on the server downloads again at its new size.
- The same title on two servers is two downloads.
- A broken list of downloads is repaired and kept, not thrown away.
- A position from an offline play does not overwrite a newer one on the server.

Settings and the server dashboard:

- A save sends only what you changed, so a setting changed elsewhere at the same time stays.
- A setting changed many times in a row is saved once, and a save that waits is written when the window closes.
- A trigger of the dashboard asks before it runs.

Pages:

- Back and Forward bring the audio and subtitle menus of the title on show.
- The page of a title keeps the audio and subtitle you picked when it loads again.
- An answer for a profile you left does not show in the next one.
- Only one popup is open at a time, the list of keys included.

## Removed

- The switches `BLOOM_OLD_PACING` and `BLOOM_AUDIO_PACING`, which gave the frame timing of older versions for a comparison. `BLOOM_FRAME_PHASE_MS=0` still gives the timing of 0.1.2.

## For developers

- The parts with no window are a crate of their own, `core/` (`bloom-core`): the Jellyfin client, the config, the downloads, the image loader, and the state machines of SyncPlay, Google Cast and the connection. Its tests run in a few seconds with no player.
- `BLOOM_TEST_SERVER=live|mock|container` chooses the server of `dev/run-test` and `dev/smoke`: your server, a mock with a fixed library, or a Jellyfin in a container.
- `dev/pacing-check` plays a clip and fails when a frame is shown late. `dev/paint-check` checks the colours of a poster in the window.
- The local changes of the vendored GPUI crates are patch files in `vendor/patches`, with `dev/vendor-crates` to check and upgrade them.
