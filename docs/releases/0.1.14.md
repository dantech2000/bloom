## New

- **Choose a version.** When a film or episode has more than one file (4K and 1080p, or two editions), its page has a Version box and the player's settings have a Version menu. The audio, subtitles and file facts follow the version you pick, and Play and Download use it.
- **Collections, playlists and studios per library.** A library's View menu shows the collections and playlists that hold its titles, and its studios. A studio opens its films and shows.
- **Included In.** A title's page lists the collections it belongs to.
- **Upcoming.** Episodes that have not aired yet can be a Home row. Add it in Settings > Home.
- **Language filters.** Filter a library by audio language or subtitle language.
- **Original language.** "Original language" is now a choice for the preferred audio: each title plays in the language it was made in.
- **Metadata in your language.** The app asks the server for names and overviews in your system's languages.
- **Are you still watching?** After a few episodes in a row with no input, the next one waits paused and asks. It is off until you choose a rule in Settings > Playback.
- **Frame by frame.** While paused, `,` and `.` step one frame back or forward.
- **The dashboard** has Backups (list, create, and restore behind a confirmation), a Follow switch that keeps a log moving as the server writes it, and activity you can filter by kind, level, user and time, with paging.

## Fixed

- **Signing in to a profile** always offers the password field. Jellyfin 12 says every account has a password, so the app no longer hides the field.
