An installed 0.1.5 updates itself: it shows "Restart to update" when the download is ready.

## Liquid glass

Menus, panels, the player controls and the round buttons on the home page are now glass that bends the picture behind it. The edge works as a lens, a thin line of light runs along it, and the glass takes the colour of what is under it. Over a bright picture it darkens by itself, so the text on it stays readable.

Settings > Display > Liquid glass turns it off and gives the frosted glass of before.

While a video plays, the glass takes a cheaper path, so a menu over a film does not slow the picture.

## At home on the Mac

- Settings opens with Cmd-, and from the Bloom menu.
- Cmd-W closes the window and Bloom stays in the Dock. A click on the Dock icon opens the window again.
- An Edit menu: Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All work in every text field.
- Forward (Cmd-]) next to Back (Cmd-[). A two-finger swipe, a three-finger swipe and the side buttons of a mouse go back and forward too.
- The full-screen item of the View menu says Enter or Exit.
- A Help menu with the README, these notes and the page to report a problem.
- Escape closes an open SyncPlay or cast panel before it goes back a page.

## Server dashboard

- The cards of the dashboard are glass too.
- Artwork in the library, plugin and session cards stays inside the round corners of its card.

## Dark only

The "Light appearance" switch is gone. Every panel, card and overlay of Bloom is made for a dark page, and the light page never looked right. If you had chosen light, Bloom is dark again after this update.

## Fixed

- Several downloads queued in a row show one notice that counts them, in place of one card for each on top of each other.
- A download that is removed takes its saved position with it. Before, a film downloaded again started where the old copy stopped, however far you had watched elsewhere since.
- The player's settings menu no longer opens as an empty card when it has no entries.

## For developers

- The tests with the real player run on GitHub Actions and fail the run, but for three that need a real GPU or an even clock (`docs/RELEASING.md`).
- `dev/jctl glass` changes the glass live: `glass help` lists the keys.
