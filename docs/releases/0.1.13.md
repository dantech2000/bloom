## New

- **Film and episode pages say more.** A Facts section gives your progress ("Resume from 42:10 · 57m left"), the file size, the audio languages and the subtitles. Directors, writers, studios and genres open their own pages. The More menu adds Play next and Add to queue, and keeps the server tools under Manage.
- **Seasons play.** A season page has Play (the next episode you have not watched, or the one you started) and Shuffle. A show's Play button names its episode ("Play S1:E2").
- **Plain track names.** Audio and subtitles read the same way everywhere: "English", "English (Forced)", "English · image", "English · 5.1". The format sits beside the name as a detail.
- **A full Playback menu.** Skip back and forward, previous and next episode, the next subtitle or audio track, mute, volume and speed are all in the menu bar, with shortcuts. Speed has a 1.75× step.
- **Library filters.** Filter by genre, decade, resolution (4K, HD, SD) and "Has subtitles". The filters in use show as pills you can click to remove.
- **Playlists and collections have a tab.** An empty list offers "New playlist" (or "New collection").
- **A Home for a new account.** Until you start something, Home shows "Pick something to watch" and a row of genres.
- **Settings > Home** is now the list of your home rows in order. You can move a row, remove it, or add one that is not on the list yet. Rows that only Jellyfin Web shows sit in a group of their own.
- **The dashboard** shows what the server is doing now (tasks, conversions and why) and what was added this week. Repeated alerts are grouped into one line with a count, and each device shows once, with this app marked. Restart and Shut Down moved into a menu beside Scan.

## Fixed

- **The heart and the watched mark show their real state.** The heart was drawn filled whether or not the title was a favourite.
- **Subtitles:** the detail page now shows the subtitle the server will start. Before, it could say English while the player opened with subtitles off.
- **The tab that lights** is the one for the page's own library. "Movies" stayed lit on search, show and season pages.
- **Opening the page you are on again** (the same episode twice) no longer adds a step to Back.
- **Home showed only the first seven rows** of your home layout. It now shows all ten.
- Menus are easier to read over bright posters and video, and the top bar frosts over when a page scrolls under it.
- Row arrows show only when a row has more to scroll. A runtime under a minute reads "20 s", not "0m". A long title in a narrow window shrinks to fit.
- Icon buttons have tooltips that say what they do.
