## New

- **A new server dashboard.** Users is now a list with a page for each user, where the actions are rows and Delete is last. A list of ten entries or more (users, tasks, logs) gets a field to find one. Plugins and tasks show one action and keep the rest in a "…" menu. Logs are grouped (the server's, the plugins', the media conversions') and show whole names. A section that cannot load says why in plain words and offers "Try again"; a section that loads shows its shape while it waits.
- **Pages move.** A new page lifts into place, and a menu or a dialog settles in over a fading background. Reduce Motion, in Bloom's settings or the system's, turns this off.
- **A library has pages.** Previous and Next under the grid, with the range on show, in place of one long scroll.
- **Casting.** Bloom finds Chromecasts through macOS's own Bonjour service, the same way it finds AirPlay receivers, and stops looking once the panel closes. It remembers each Chromecast's certificate. While playing to an AirPlay receiver, the audio and subtitle tracks can be changed.

## Fixed

- **SyncPlay:** a change to the group's queue no longer stops the drift correction or Pause of the item that plays. Leaving a group gives the player back its normal speed and controls.
- **Quitting during playback tells the server playback stopped,** so the item keeps the right position on other devices.
- **The end of one item could stop the next one while it loaded.** It no longer does.
- **Choosing a track** from the detail page, from a remote control and with the Jellyfin Enhanced keys now goes the same way in all three. One of them could pick the wrong track when the server streamed a file without re-encoding it.
- A server answer larger than 10 MB was cut short without an error. A connection that never answers now gives up instead of waiting.
- **Downloads:** when the list of downloads is lost, Bloom finds the files in their folders again. A full disk stops a download instead of retrying it.
- **Signing out or switching servers** stops casting and clears the list of devices.
