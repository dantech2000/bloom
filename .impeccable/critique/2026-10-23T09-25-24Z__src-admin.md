## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Authored for this product. The groups are Jellyfin's own, the library cards carry the library's art, the server card puts Scan, Restart and Shut Down where an administrator looks first, and the phone's list of sections is the same structure in native clothes. The one departure from the committed world is the admin area's blue-to-purple wash: deliberate, but it bleeds into the card edges, which pick up an indigo tint instead of the glass edge, and on the phone the four stat pills have clearly blue outlines.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

A capable dashboard with the right bones: the landing page answers "is the server fine?" in one look, and every dangerous thing asks first in plain words. What it lacks is the second level of thought for scale and for the phone. Ten users are ten identical cards with four buttons each; forty tasks are forty rows with a Run button and an unlabelled icon; thirty-five logs are thirty-five tiles with their names cut in the middle. On the phone the Users page becomes six screens of scrolling. The biggest opportunity: give each list a compact row form and move the per-item actions one level down.

## What's Working

- The landing page: status badge in amber, six counts, active devices, activity beside alerts, storage bars. Scannable in two seconds; colour only for state.
- Destructive actions: "Restart the server?", "Disable cole?" with plain consequences. Shut Down is the only red fill on the page.
- Settings pages: grouped cards, a one-line explanation under every field, a Save and Discard bar only when something changed, a toast that names a refusal.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] The Users page does not scale, and collapses on the phone. Ten full cards with avatar, three facts and four buttons each; on the phone each card is a full screen, the stat pills take the top third, and there is no search. Fix: a compact row per user with the facts and actions on the user's own page, a search field above the list once there are more than six, the stat pills as one line. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt src/admin/users.rs
- [P1] Every list repeats its actions on every row (users, plugins, tasks). Fix: one primary action per row at most and the rest in a "…" menu, Delete and Uninstall last in the danger register. Suggested command: /impeccable distill src/admin/
- [P1] A page that fails to load shows the raw error and no way back ("Could not load this page: {error}", "Loading…"). Fix: a plain sentence per cause with a Retry button, and a skeleton while loading. Suggested command: /impeccable harden src/admin/mod.rs
- [P2] The wash tints the glass edges: cards and the phone's stat pills show an indigo border instead of the glass edge. Fix: keep the wash, draw edges as Screen White at 20%. Suggested command: /impeccable polish src/admin/
- [P2] Names and descriptions truncate with nothing behind them: task descriptions end in "missi…", log names lose their middle, the Logs page is a 3×12 grid of equal tiles. Fix: tasks wrap or reveal their description on tap; logs become a list grouped by kind with full names, the server log first. Suggested command: /impeccable layout src/admin/logs.rs

## Persona Red Flags

- Alex (Power User): no search on Users, Tasks or Logs; no keyboard shortcut to Save, Run or a section; Enter in a prompt does not appear to submit; no bulk action.
- Sam (Accessibility): buttons carry roles and labels; the clock button on a task has no label; 12-point detail text at 50% ink on the wash sits near the contrast floor; status dots always have a word beside them.
- Casey (Distracted, on the phone): New user and the back arrow sit at the top, out of the thumb's reach; the cards' four identical buttons need reading before tapping.
- The household admin on the phone (from PRODUCT.md): restart and "is the scan done" are one screen away; "let mom sign in again" means scrolling through ten tall cards.

## Minor Observations

- Settings fields look like static text with a pencil until clicked.
- Superseded plugin cards sit at full weight beside their replacement.
- "Open settings in the web" hands the phone user to Safari.
- Shut Down sits beside Scan All Libraries on the landing page.
- Cognitive load moderate: two checklist failures (choices per decision point, chunking on the phone).

## Questions to Consider

- Does an administrator on the phone need the whole dashboard, or the five things that go wrong at home?
- If the wash stayed and every edge went back to the glass edge, would the dashboard still feel like its own place?
