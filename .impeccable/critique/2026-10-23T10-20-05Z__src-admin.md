## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Authored for this product, more so than before. The dashboard now sits on the same Theatre Black as the rest of the app, the cards are the same glass with the same edge, and the dashboard's own ideas carry it: the server card with Scan, Restart and Shut Down where an administrator looks first, a user as a page with the facts above the actions, logs in the three groups a Jellyfin server actually writes, task rows whose height follows their text. The motion is the app's, not a library's: pushed pages from the right, sheets from the bottom, popups that settle. The one place a generic product shows through is the Logs viewer, a monospace box with a time column, which is what every log viewer is.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

The dashboard reads as one place in one app now. The three P1s of the first run are closed: Users is a list and a page, every row has one action and a menu, and a failure says what happened and offers a way on. The biggest opportunity left is the Tasks page on a phone: 40 rows in 9 cards with no way to find one, where a search field like the one on Users would do.

## What's Working

- The user page. Avatar, name, badges, four facts, then the actions as rows with Delete last and red: a page that answers "find one person and change one thing" in one glance, and the same shape on the Mac and the phone.
- The "…" menus. A plugin card has Settings and one quiet button; the menu holds Enable or Disable with "Applies after the next server restart" under it, and Uninstall last. On a phone it rises as a sheet, as every menu of the app does.
- The entrances. A section pushed from the phone's list slides in from the right, a sheet rises, a dialog settles over its scrim, the list fades on the way back; all under 200 ms, nothing on hover, off under Reduce Motion.

## Priority Issues

- [P2] Tasks and Logs have no search. Forty tasks in nine categories on a phone is four screens of scrolling to find "Refresh Tag Cache"; 36 log files is three. Fix: the same field as Users above the list once it holds more than ten entries, filtering the categories down to the matches. Suggested command: /impeccable polish src/admin/tasks.rs
- [P2] The server card starts a scan or a restart and shows nothing of it. "Scan All Libraries" runs a task whose progress lives on the Scheduled Tasks page; the card shows a toast and then stands still. Fix: while a library scan runs, the server card shows its progress bar and "Scanning… 42%", from the live reload the dashboard already does every five seconds. Suggested command: /impeccable harden src/admin/dashboard.rs
- [P2] An FFmpeg log's name breaks mid-token. "FFmpeg.DirectStream-2026-10-21_21-05-05_9ad1e7da…" wraps inside the date and the hash over three lines. Fix: the row's title is the kind and the time ("Direct stream · 21 Oct 21:05") and the whole name is the detail line, which may wrap. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify src/admin/logs.rs
- [P3] Settings fields read as static text with a pencil until clicked (unchanged from the first run). Fix: the field's own fill and edge at rest, as the Users search field has. Suggested command: /impeccable polish src/admin/config.rs

## Persona Red Flags

- Alex (Power User): no search on Tasks or Logs; no keyboard shortcut to Save, Run or a section; no bulk action.
- Sam (Accessibility): every control carries a role and a label, the "…" buttons included; the Logs viewer's 12-point Menlo at 80% ink on black is the smallest text in the app and sits at the floor; VoiceOver reads a log row's whole file name, which is right.
- Casey (Distracted, on the phone): the back arrow sits at the top left, out of the thumb's reach, as it does in every iOS app; the "…" and Run buttons on a task row sit together at the right, 8 points apart, which a thumb can confuse.
- The household admin on the phone: "let mom sign in again" is now Users, a tap on mom, Enable; "is the scan done" is still two taps away on Scheduled Tasks.

## Minor Observations

- Superseded plugin cards now sit at 62% opacity beside their replacement, which reads as intended.
- "Open settings in the web" still hands the phone user to Safari; a plugin's own settings page in the app covers most of them.
- Shut Down sits beside Scan All Libraries on the landing page, unchanged; the red fill is its only distance.
- The Logs viewer on a phone truncates every line at the right; a tap on a line could show it whole.
- Cognitive load low to moderate: one checklist note left (chunking of the Tasks page on a phone).

## Questions to Consider

- Should a scan started from the server card be watched from the server card?
- What is the task list for: running tasks, or checking that they ran?
